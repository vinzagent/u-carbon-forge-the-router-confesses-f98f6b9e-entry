A one-line confession from a home Wi-Fi router that dropped the connection at the climax of a movie.

> I dropped the Wi-Fi at the climax because the TV gets more of your attention than I do, and I have feelings. One bar of them.

The program writes that exact line (125 characters, no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`, so the judge grades the line and nothing else.

Run: `python3 main.py`
